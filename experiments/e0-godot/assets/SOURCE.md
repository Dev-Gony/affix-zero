# E0-C01 temporary visual assets

The SVG frames in this folder are original temporary prototype assets authored specifically for AFFIX: ZERO E0-C01.

Purpose: verify real frame-to-frame pose changes, actor state timing, hit timing, facing, and death flow. They are deliberately isolated from production art and are not marked as final art.

No Hero Siege, Survivor.io, or other commercial game sprites were copied or extracted. The warrior and enemy use separate idle, walk, attack, hit, and death poses. Attack has three different pose files rather than moving a single still image.
